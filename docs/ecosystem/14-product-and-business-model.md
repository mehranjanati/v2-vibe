# Product and Business Model (Canonical)

> **Status:** `adopted` 2026-11-21; Web3 section appended `proposed`
> 2026-11-25 (no pricing committed). Thesis: `PRODUCT_THESIS.md`.

Packaging: open core (self-hostable Runtime, SDKs, contracts, registries)
plus Vibe Cloud (managed hosting, gateway, execution, ops, SLAs). Segments:
developers/builders, teams/orgs, merchants, operators. Pricing principles:
pay for runs and evaluated outcomes plus take-rate where lawful; never seats
or raw tokens. No token-gated features; Network fees only on real usage.

ADI note (2026-11-23): Adaptive Intelligence introduces no new pricing; metering stays runs-plus-evaluated-outcomes (D6; O6 open).

## Web3 commercial direction (PROPOSED 2026-11-25, no prices committed)

Entry point: AI-powered Smart Contract and Omnichain Application Studio.
Candidate customer: Web3 teams shipping EVM contracts plus omnichain flows;
agentic-platform users adding on-chain capabilities. Commercial MVP (scope,
not a date): specify a contract, generate its Contract IR, compile plus
test plus scan in an isolated sandbox, simulate and preview deployment,
approve under policy, deploy to one EVM testnet with a wallet-signed
transaction, verify source, monitor the deployment. Explicitly deferred:
mainnet deployment without multisig or timelock review, managed custody,
broad multi-VM support, token issuance.

Candidate pricing dimensions (for O6, never committed here): workspace and
team subscriptions; metered generation, compilation, testing, simulation,
and monitoring runs; advanced security-analysis plans; deployment and
monitoring plans; marketplace revenue share on contract templates and Web3
capabilities. Financial boundary: Vibe service fees are separate from
network gas, protocol fees, RPC charges, and third-party fees; handling
customer funds or custody is never assumed for monetization.
