# Product Vision — Ecosystem Framing (Canonical delta)

> **Status:** `adopted` 2026-11-21. Extends `00-product-vision.md` without
> rewriting it. Full definitions: `ecosystem/01-product-ecosystem.md`.

Vibe is one open, model-agnostic, self-hostable ecosystem of eight products
sharing identity, security, and contracts: Assistant, Research, Coder,
Commerce, Studio, Runtime, Cloud, Network. Open by default under MIT;
commercially viable without a token; protocols as adapters; community
extensions versioned and signed.
